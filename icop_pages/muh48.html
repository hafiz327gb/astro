When to Observe Muharram Waxing (NEW) Crescent ?


The geocentric conjunction (Geocentric New Moon) will occur Inshalla on (Monday 15 June 2026) at 02:54 UT.

Sighting the new crescent on (Monday 15 June 2026) and (Tuesday 16 June 2026) is shown in the below graphs using the program Accurate Times by Mohammad Odeh according to Odeh criterion. Where:-

  • It is impossible to see the crescent from the areas located under the red color. Because either the Moon on this day sets before the Sunset and/or the topocentric conjunction occurs after the Sunset.
  • The crescent is expected to be seen by optical aid only from the areas located under the blue color.
  • The crescent is expected to be seen by optical aid from the areas located under the magenta color. In these areas the crescent could be seen by naked eye if the atmospheric conditions are superb and the observer is experienced.
  • The crescent is expected to be easily visible by naked eye from the areas located under the green color.
  • The crescent cannot be seen from uncolored areas, even though the Moon sets in these locations after the Sunset and the topocentric conjunction occurs before the Sunset, but the Moon is not sufficiently illuminated in order to be seen as crescent even by optical aid.
  • Kindly notice that the below graph shows the possibility of seeing the crescent from areas between 60 degrees north of Equator down to 60 degrees south of Equator.

Monday 15 June 2026


Tuesday 16 June 2026

According to the Universal Hejric Calendar (UHC), which is based on the calculated crescent visibility, the start of this month in the Eastern Region will be on Tuesday 16 June 2026 and in the Western Region will be on Tuesday 16 June 2026. Kindly notice that the UHC is a pre-calculated calendar, which adopts a certain criterion to start the new Hejric month. Your country/organization might adopt different criterion to start the new Hejric month. So it is highly advised to read the UHC website before giving any judgment.

The event involved active participation from female students (santriwati) who are active members of CASA (Club Astronomi Santri Assalaam) , as part of the observatory's educational mission to integrate practical astronomy into Assalaam Islamic boarding school curricula.

2. Astronomical Data & Ephemeris
According to the precise mathematical computations (hisab) generated by the Assalaam Observatory team, the astronomical circumstances for the observation site are detailed below:
• Conjunction (Ijtimak) Time: Monday, June 15, 2026, at 09:56:00 WIB
• Sunset (Maghrib): 17:27:59 WIB (Azimuth: 293.42°)
• Moonset: 17:40:41 WIB (Azimuth: 298.22°)
• Crescent Altitude (Height): 2.77°
• Elongation: 6.37°
• Moon Age at Sunset: 07 hours 31 minutes 59 seconds
• Moon Azimuth: 298.66°

3. Observation Results & Meteorological Conditions
The field observation was carried out from the rooftop observation deck of Assalaam Observatory, utilizing optical instruments including a refractor telescope. The santriwati from CASA, alongside their instructors, carefully monitored the western horizon where the hilal was theoretically calculated to be positioned slightly to the upper right of the setting sun.

Conclusion of Visibility:
The crescent moon (hilal) was NOT visible during the entire duration of the session due to the following decisive constraints:
1. Weather Conditions: The western horizon was heavily obscured by dense cloud cover, severely blocking the light path from the faint crescent.
2. Danjon Limit Infringement: Astronomically, the calculated elongation of the moon stood at only 6.37°, with a low altitude of 2.77°. This puts the crescent well below the Danjon Limit (typically established at 7° elongation), below which the crescent is physically incapable of reflecting sufficient sunlight to form a visually or optically detectable continuous line of light.

4. Summary
​Due to the combination of unfavorable local weather and the intrinsic astronomical limitations imposed by the Danjon Limit, a positive sighting of the Muharram 1448 H hilal could not be achieved by the CASA members at this specific station. 

​However, according to the New MABIMS criteria adopted by the Ministry of Religious Affairs of the Republic of Indonesia—which requires a minimum crescent altitude of 3° and a minimum elongation of 6.4°—the parameters for the beginning of the month had already been successfully fulfilled in the westernmost region of Indonesia, specifically in Aceh. Consequently, according to the Government of Indonesia, the 1st of Muharram 1448 H is officially declared to commence on Tuesday, June 16, 2026.
